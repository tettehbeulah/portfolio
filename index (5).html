Who I Am

About Me

My marketing journey began at Accra Technical University, where I studied Marketing and developed a strong foundation in consumer behaviour, branding, market research, and communication. Since then, I have been deepening that foundation with dedicated digital marketing training and hands-on practice — building strategies, creating content, studying analytics, and testing ideas on real brands.

I'm especially drawn to social media marketing, SEO, content creation, paid advertising, marketing analytics, and the growing role of AI in digital marketing. I enjoy the balance between the creative side of marketing and the measurable side — the story and the numbers.

Alongside marketing, I run Beulahland Croché, my handmade crochet brand — an experience that shapes how I approach branding, content, and customers today.

“I believe effective marketing starts with understanding people, creating value, and using data to make smarter decisions.”

Research

The Impact of Email Personalisation on Customer Engagement

A research project examining how personalised email communication influences customer engagement in Ghana's banking sector — looking at relevance, timing, and tone, and how they shape customer response and loyalty.

Themes Explored
  • Email Personalisation
  • Customer Engagement
  • Relevant Messaging
  • Customer Loyalty
  • Data Analysis
  • Email Marketing
Objectives
  • Assess how personalised messaging affects open and response behaviour.
  • Understand what customers consider relevant vs. intrusive communication.
  • Link personalisation practices to longer-term customer loyalty.
Key Insight
  • Relevance matters more than frequency — messages tied to a customer's actual needs consistently drive stronger engagement than generic broadcasts.
